About

Jonathan Okon is an HR and Administrative Support Professional based in Abuja, Nigeria, specializing in recruitment coordination, employee onboarding, HR documentation, and executive support. Trained as an electrical engineer, Jonathan discovered his strength in people management while leading a team of labourers during an engineering internship, and made a deliberate move into HR and administration.

Today he supports management at Razley Prints, where he redesigned the intern and NYSC onboarding workflow, maintains organized employee records, and keeps scheduling and daily priorities on track. Previously, as Program Coordinator at Priority Groups, he owned recruitment pipelines from sourcing to interview scheduling.

With a process-driven engineering mindset, Jonathan Okon brings structure and reliability to every team he joins. He holds more than 40 professional certificates in HR, project management, and leadership, including courses recognized for HRCI, SHRM, PMI, and NASBA credit, and works fluently across Google Workspace, Asana, Trello, and Microsoft Office. Jonathan Okon is open to HR coordinator, recruitment coordinator, and administrative support roles, locally and internationally.
